# Our Story

A quiet record of the years — the moments, the places, the people we became together.

---

## The beginning

Every story needs a first line. Ours started with something small: a glance held a second too long, a laugh that surprised both of us. We did not know yet what it was. We know now.

---

## The years that followed

Some years are loud. Full of firsts — first trips, first fights, first mornings waking up and choosing each other all over again. Other years are softer. A rhythm settles in, comfortable and true.

These pages hold both kinds.

---

## What we keep

Not just the beautiful photographs. Also the blurry ones taken in bad light. The note written on a napkin. The playlist that takes us straight back to that summer. These are the things worth keeping.
